# IXD-404 W7 — Toolbars

Two toolbar systems for the Happy Earth Corp console, each with its own tone.

- **Train** (`index.html`): each carriage is a button. The landscape scrolls past while the train holds still, and each car reacts in its own way when tapped.
- **Cards** (`cards.html`): a hand of playing cards. The card you play flips over and becomes the info box, styled by its suit.

Both pages support left, center and right alignment, and show enabled, hover and pressed states. Scroll down (or press **SPEC ↓**) to see the dimension sheets.

Live: https://wbgrimm.github.io/ixd404-week7-toolbar/
